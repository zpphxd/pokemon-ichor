The Aethos Pokédex plan

One hundred and fifty new Pokémon, each a memory of a Greek myth, laid out as a real region: three starters, route birds and bugs, a mascot, fossils, a pseudo-legendary and the legendaries of the Loom. This is the plan to approve before the full entries and the art.

The catalog

All 150 slots

The labels are working concepts, not names. Names, stats, abilities, moves and Pokédex entries come at Gate 2.

Source: design/dex/plan.yaml on the claude/aethos-dex-a7c73a branch. tools/dex/plan.py checks it and rebuilds this page and design/dex/PLAN.md.

Costs to know: 150 species with front, back, shiny, icon, footprint and follower sprites come to roughly 1.2–1.5 MB of ROM if they reuse existing cries, about a quarter of today's headroom. The plan needs no new moves; a few signature moves may be proposed at Gate 2, each flagged as a cost.
