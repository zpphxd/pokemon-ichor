II · The story

Fate against free will

A story about hubris and what it costs. It has a rival with a real arc, a villain you meet as a kind man before you know what he is, and a climax where the whole region stops.

You, the Unwoven

Protagonist · Theo or Ianthe

On the night you were born the Oracle went blind for an hour. She has never been able to see your thread.

Professor Daphne

Philosopher of the Akademia grove

Believes every myth is a memory of a Pokémon. Professors are named for trees, and the daphne is the laurel.

Alethea

Friend · her name means truth

Daphne's apprentice. She keeps the Pokédex and digs through ruins for what the prophecies leave out.

Kleon

Rival · from kleos, glory

Raised in the Lakonian battle school. A prophecy promised him a short, glorious life, and he runs at it. Hubris, a fall, and a way back.

Team Titan

The Titanborn

Bronze Corinthian helmets and a sickle crest. Admins Hyperion, Iapetos the forger of prophecies, and Rhea. They fight with earth-born Rock, Ground and Steel.

Kronos

Masked leader · secretly Phaedon

A gentle healer whose daughter Elpis, named for Hope, is fated to die young. He would freeze the world in a golden age where no thread is ever cut.

Act I

The Unwoven · Laurels 1–3

  • A coming-of-age festival, your first Pokémon, your first fight with Kleon.
  • Titan grunts dig in hilltop ruins for a fragment of the Harpe, the old sickle of Kronos.
  • A trireme to the mainland. In the Mystery sanctum of Eleusa, the Titans take the first fragment.
  • Daphne tells you what you are. The Fates weave every life on a Loom, and yours was never spun.
Act II

Hubris · Laurels 4–6

  • At the Dionysia, Iapetos stages a tragedy that entrances a whole amphitheater.
  • Crushed by the Huntress Kallisto, Kleon lets Iapetos rewrite his fate and joins the Titans.
  • In the capital you meet a kind healer and his sick daughter. Then the Titans storm the Akropolis.
  • At the volcano forge you learn only someone without a thread can hold the Harpe. That means you.
Act III

The Loom · Laurels 7–8

  • Into the Labyrinth to Team Titan's base. The mask comes off. It is the healer.
  • At Delphoi the Fates are found in chains, bound there by the first Archon. You cut them free, and Kronos takes their power. Dialga awakens.
  • The Frozen Hour. All of Aethos stops, drained to gray. Only you move. Rouse Uxie, Mesprit and Azelf, then climb the Stair of Hours with Kleon at your side.
  • Then the Ascent of Olympus, the Pantheon, and the Archon.

V · Systems

Modern under the marble

Apotheosis

Mega Evolution, including the Legends Z-A Megas. The key stone is the Omphalos and Mega Stones are Ichor Stones. It is the one battle gimmick. Z-Moves, Dynamax and Tera stay off.

Mortal, Hero, Spartan

Three difficulties. Spartan brings level caps, set mode, no items in battle, smarter AI and competitive boss teams. The optional Oath of the Styx is a sworn Nuzlocke ruleset.

Relics, not HM slaves

Talaria sandals to run, Daedalus's Wings to fly, Poseidon's Conch to surf, the Labrys to cut, the Bronze Hammer, the Girdle of Herakles and Hecate's Torch.

The 2026 checklist

Followers, day and night with time-based encounters, the Augur's Eye (DexNav), a Scroll of Deeds quest log, running indoors, reusable TMs, EV and IV views, mints, the party move relearner, menu access to storage, and fast text and battle options.

VI · After the credits

A post-game bigger than the story

The Twelve Labors

A quest chain of retold myths: the Nemean Lion (Pyroar), the Hydra (Hydreigon), the Golden Hind (Xerneas), the Stymphalian Birds (Corviknight), the Apples of the Hesperides (Hydrapple), Cerberus (Mega Houndoom), and six more.

Katabasis

Pay Charon an obol to cross the Styx, home of Basculegion. Pass Cerberus, walk the Asphodel Meadows and Elysium, free the Titans in Tartarus, then face Hades, Persephone and Giratina.

The Olympiad

The Battle Frontier at Olympia. Its Frontier Brains are heroes: Achilles, Atalanta, Odysseus and Hippolyta. An Arena of Champions hosts rematches with every Hierophant.

The Odyssey

A voyage over the wine-dark sea: the Cyclops's cave, Circe's island, the Sirens, Scylla and Charybdis, the Lotus-Eaters and Aeolus's bag of winds. It ends with a homecoming to Elaia.

Atlantis

A sunken marble city guarded by Manaphy, Kyogre and Lugia.

The Prime Mover

Groudon as Gaia, Kyogre as Oceanus and Rayquaza as Ouranos. Last comes Arceus on the true summit of Olympus.

VII · Look and sound

A world painted on pottery

The interface

Text boxes and menus carry terracotta and black meander borders like the bands on this page. Battle intros are black-figure silhouettes, the title screen is a painted amphora, and the trainer card is a kylix cup.

The overworld

Faithful Gen 3 pixel art on 16-pixel tiles, in full color. Olive groves, vineyards, Cycladic villages, marble cities, cypress forests, a volcanic caldera, the Labyrinth, snow above the clouds on Olympus, the gray Underworld and golden Elysium.

The music

Written in the original Greek modes, Dorian, Phrygian, Lydian and Mixolydian, on lyre, kithara, aulos and frame drum. Battles run in Greek folk meters, 7/8 kalamatianos and 9/8 zeibekiko. The Frozen Hour is near silence and a ticking music box.

VIII · How we build it

One director, a team of specialist agents

The game is built on pokeemerald-expansion 1.17.2, the modern Emerald decompilation, so every map, script and sprite is source code under version control. A creative director session owns the vision and reviews everything. Specialist agents work in parallel inside it.

X · Risks

What could sink it, and the plan for each

AI-content bans

PokéCommunity's 2026 rules ban AI-generated art and writing, and Eevee Expo reportedly does too. A hack built by agents may need its own release channels, or human artists and an editor for final assets. This needs a decision before public release, not now.

Art is the bottleneck

Generated images do not survive the GBA's 16-color tiles without heavy cleanup. We plan palettes first, compile tilesets with Porytiles, use credited free resources, and approve the look at the Phase 1 gate before building more.

Legal exposure

Nintendo has taken down standalone fan games. Patch-only hacks have mostly been left alone. We stay non-commercial, ship only a patch, and players bring their own Emerald ROM.

Cartridge space

A GBA cartridge tops out at 32 MB. We measure a full-species build in Phase 0 and budget art and music against it.

Fun needs a human

Crash and flow tests can be automated. Whether a fight feels fair, or a route drags, needs your hands on the controls at each phase gate.

Your calls

Four decisions

  1. The title. Pokémon Ichor is the working title. Pokémon Laurel is the open alternate.
  2. The starters. Snivy, Cyndaquil and Piplup, three generations and three myths, none overlapping Lazarus.
  3. The go-ahead for Phase 0. The compiler installer needs your Mac password once. Everything after that runs here.
  4. Release policy on AI content. Not needed until we near a public release.
