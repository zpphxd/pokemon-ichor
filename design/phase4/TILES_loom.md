# Tileset: loom

`gTileset_Loom` and `gTileset_StairOfHours` (secondaries, on the oikos primary): the Loom under the Omphalos and the
Stair of Hours (`design/phase4/SCRIPT.md` III-9 to III-11; briefs in `design/maps/TheLoom.md`
and `design/maps/StairOfHours.md`). Built by `tools/ichor/art/loom_build.py` from
`tools/ichor/art/loom_art.py`; this file is generated.

## How it is made

Both maps are one-off set pieces, so each is **painted whole as a scene** in every state
the story needs, then sliced into 16x16 cells; every distinct cell is one metatile, and
`tools/ichor/maps/climax.py` lays each map from the same grid. Repeating ground (warp,
floor, chasm, sky, paving) is painted on patterns locked to the 16 px grid, so it costs a
few metatiles; the set pieces are drawn once. Scripts swap states with the setmetatile
lists in `data/scripts/ichor_climax_tiles.inc` (generated by climax.py).

| Scene | Map | What it shows |
|---|---|---|
| `loom_before` | `MAP_THE_LOOM` | The night of the Omphalos: the cloth, the warp, the three chains, the chasm |
| `loom_after` | `MAP_THE_LOOM` | After the cut: broken chains coiled on the spurs, the roof torn open, the Stair's foot on the west wall |
| `stair` | `MAP_STAIR_OF_HOURS` | Every flight solid |
| `stair_ghost` | `MAP_STAIR_OF_HOURS` | The gated flights (to landings 1, 5, 9) unformed |
| `stair_fallen` | `MAP_STAIR_OF_HOURS` | After the crumble: the hour-stones hang alone |

## Palettes (slots 6-12)

| Group | Colors | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| rock | 10 | the cavern: roof, walls, shelf floor, spurs, pillars, the chasm (`gTileset_Loom`) |
| loom | 11 | the posts, the cloth beam, bronze collars and pegs (`gTileset_Loom`) |
| warp | 13 | the threads and the dark behind them, the red knots, the woven band and its black-figure pictures, the clay loom-weights (`gTileset_Loom`) |
| chain | 5 | black adamant chains, whole and broken (middle layer) (`gTileset_Loom`) |
| stone | 12 | the Stair: cyclopean (hours 1-4), cut limestone (5-8), marble (9-11), gold-veined marble (12), the dials and numerals (`gTileset_Loom` and `gTileset_StairOfHours`) |
| sky | 10 | the stopped dawn sky, the cloud sea, the unformed steps (`gTileset_Loom` and `gTileset_StairOfHours`) |

Two secondaries, so each map has 504 tiles to itself: `gTileset_Loom` 170 metatiles and 297 tiles, `gTileset_StairOfHours` 309 metatiles and 417 tiles.
The Frozen Hour grays every palette (engine E1)
and the twelve chimes bring the color back (E6), so the Stair is drawn in its true dawn colors.
