# The Oikoumene: where every Pokémon lives

Generated by `design/oikoumene/coverage.py` from the expansion's species data. Edit the script's tables, not this file. The rules are in `design/OIKOUMENE.md` §8; legendary quests are in `design/LEGENDS.md`.

Areas marked LABORS's or ODYSSEY's are those docs' maps on the Oikoumene chart. Their own wild tables (LABORS §3, ODYSSEY §4 and §6) already hold some existing species; the homes listed here join those tables, and every species here has at least one home whatever those tables hold.

## The encounter areas

Each area's wild forms, by method. "Day" and "night" are the land table's morning-and-day and evening-and-night sets (`OW_TIME_OF_DAY_ENCOUNTERS`). A Gen 3 table holds 12 land, 5 surf, 5 rock-smash and 10 fishing slots per time of day, plus 3 Augur's Eye (DexNav) slots, so no area here is over capacity.

"Shares" are species whose home is a neighboring area of the same land and type, used to fill the rest of the table's slots so no map feels thin. A species' home is where it is most common; a share is where it also turns up.
