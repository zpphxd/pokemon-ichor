# Aethos regional dex: Gate 1 plan

Generated by `tools/dex/plan.py` from `design/dex/plan.yaml`. Edit the YAML, not this file.

The labels below are working concepts, not names. Names, stats, abilities, moves and Pokédex entries come at Gate 2, after this plan is approved.

## At a glance

- **150 species**: 11 three-stage lines, 42 two-stage lines, 1 branching line and 30 single-stage species.
- **87 dual-type** and **63 single-type** species.
- **Babies:** Storm kid, Erote.
- **Targets for Gate 2:** starters about 310 / 405 / 530 BST; the pseudo-legendary 300 / 420 / 600; Kronos and Kairos about 680; the Moirai about 580 each; Pegasus about 580; the mythicals 600.
- **Names:** the engine allows 12 characters (`POKEMON_NAME_LENGTH`); we aim for Gen 3's 10. Categories fit in 12.
- **ROM:** front, back, shiny, icon, footprint and follower sprites for 150 species come to roughly 1.2–1.5 MB if they reuse existing cries, about a quarter of today's headroom.
- **Moves:** the plan needs no new moves. Any signature move proposed at Gate 2 is flagged as a cost.

## Decisions for you

**D1. Regional starters replace Snivy, Cyndaquil and Piplup.** The plan gives Aethos its own three: the Laurel fawn (Grass, ends Grass/Fairy), the Hearth pup (Fire, ends Fire/Ghost) and the Sea foal (Water, ends Water/Ground). Phase 1 currently hands out Snivy, Cyndaquil and Piplup, and Daphne's intro speech foreshadows them ("a serpent… a giant breathing fire… a trident"). Swapping means rewriting about six lines of the slice script once the species exist in the ROM.

*Recommendation:* Yes. Keep the existing three in the slice until the new species are implemented, then swap.

*Answer:* Yes (2026-11-14): the regional starters replace Snivy, Cyndaquil and Piplup.

**D2. New story legendaries replace Dialga and the lake trio.** Kronos, Lord of Time, would replace Dialga at the climax, and the three Moirai (Spinner, Allotter, Cutter) would replace Uxie, Mesprit and Azelf. The beats stay the same: the Fates chained at the Loom, the Frozen Hour, the Stair of Hours. Only the species change. This touches the bible's §2 and §4, which the main session owns.

*Recommendation:* Yes. Without this, the region's own legendaries would sit outside its story.

*Answer:* open

**D3. Add Kairos as a post-game legendary at Olympia.** Kairos is the counterpart to Kronos: the chosen moment against endless time. A real altar of Kairos stood at the stadium entrance at Olympia, so he would wait there in the post-game Olympiad. This adds one encounter to the bible's post-game.

*Recommendation:* Yes.

*Answer:* open

**D4. Two mythicals, Hope and Ichor.** Hope is the light left in Pandora's jar. A natural beat is Elpis opening the last sealed pithos after the League, which would be a small bible addition. Ichor, the gods' golden blood, is the title Pokémon and Aethos's Mew, found at the Pool of Mnemosyne.

*Recommendation:* Yes to both. Whether Elpis gets the Hope beat is the main session's call.

*Answer:* open

**D5. How the 150 mix with the existing 1,025 species.** The bible leaves this open. Options. (a) Aethos-only through the League, with the national dex opening in the post-game (Time Fractures, the Odyssey, Atlantis, Katabasis), so "all 1,025 catchable" still holds. (b) Mixed throughout. (c) Aethos-only, full stop. The Phase 1 Route 1 table uses existing species today.

*Recommendation:* (a). It makes Aethos feel like a real new region and keeps the bible's post-game promise.

*Answer:* (b) mixed (2026-11-14): the 150 are Aethos's own Pokémon, and existing species live in the region alongside them.

**D6. Let the post-game Labors use regional species.** The bible casts the Twelve Labors with existing species. Where the dex has its own, they could take over: Nemean lion (1), Hydra (2), Calydonian boar (4), Stymphalian birds (6), the two-headed hound for Geryon's cattle (10), Ladon (11), and the starter line's three-headed hound for Cerberus (12). The Hind, the Augean Stables, the Cretan Bull, Diomedes's mares and Hippolyta's girdle keep their current casting.

*Recommendation:* Yes, where a regional species exists.

*Answer:* open

## The lines: myth, design and habitat

### Starters

#### #001–003 Laurel fawn (Grass) → Laurel doe (Grass) → Laurel dryad (Grass/Fairy)

- **Role:** starter
- **Myth:** Daphne, a river nymph, fled Apollo after Eros struck him with a gold arrow and her with a lead one. Ovid has her run "as the deer runs from the lion". She prayed to be changed and became the laurel, Apollo's tree, whose wreaths crown every victor (Ovid, Metamorphoses 1).
- **Design:** A fawn with laurel-leaf ears, then a doe-nymph running on two legs with bark creeping up her shins, then the moment of change: rooted legs, branching arms, and a living laurel crown like the one the player earns.
- **Lives in:** Akademia grove, Elaia (starter). The other two return at the post-game Sanctuary of Firsts.

#### #004–006 Hearth pup (Fire) → Two-headed hound (Fire) → Three-headed gatehound (Fire/Ghost)

- **Role:** starter
- **Myth:** Kerberos, the hound at the gate of Hades, and his two-headed brother Orthros (Hesiod, Theogony). Herakles dragged him up at Cape Tainaron for his twelfth Labor. The Phlegethon, the underworld's river of fire, runs past his gate.
- **Design:** A pup with one head awake and two sleepy heads tucked into its ruff. One more head wakes at each stage: two at the middle stage (Orthros), three at the last, with a Phlegethon-fire mane and a serpent tail. Each head learns one fang: Fire Fang, Thunder Fang, Ice Fang.
- **Lives in:** Akademia grove, Elaia (starter).

#### #007–009 Sea foal (Water) → Hippocamp (Water) → Earth-shaker steed (Water/Ground)

- **Role:** starter
- **Myth:** Poseidon Hippios, lord of horses, drove a chariot of hippocampi, horses with fish tails. He was Ennosigaios, the Earth-shaker, god of earthquakes as well as the sea, and in one story he struck a rock and made the first horse.
- **Design:** A foal with a fin mane and a curled fish tail that splashes in tide pools, then a sleek sea-horse, then a powerful steed whose hooves crack the seabed, with a mane like a breaking wave.
- **Lives in:** Akademia grove, Elaia (starter).

### Kalliste Isle

#### #010–012 Hoopoe chick (Normal/Flying) → Hoopoe (Normal/Flying) → King of birds (Normal/Flying)

- **Role:** route bird
- **Myth:** In Aristophanes's Birds, Tereus the hoopoe (Epops) talks the birds into building Cloudcuckooland, a city in the clouds that cuts the gods off from mortals' offerings.
- **Design:** The hoopoe's fan crest grows into a crown. The final stage is the self-made king of birds, and its nests are woven from cloud.
- **Lives in:** Route 1 and Elaia's olive groves (chick); mainland roads (hoopoe); the Ascent's cliffs (king).

#### #013–014 Weasel kit (Normal) → Laughing weasel (Normal)

- **Role:** common mammal
- **Myth:** Galanthis, Alkmene's servant, tricked the birth goddess Eileithyia into letting Herakles be born, then laughed at her. She was turned into a weasel, and Hecate took her in as an attendant (Ovid, Metamorphoses 9).
- **Design:** A quick weasel whose face markings make a permanent grin. It carries what it finds in its mouth. The grown form wears Hecate's dark collar.
- **Lives in:** Everywhere on Kalliste and the mainland farms; most common at night.

#### #015–017 Soul caterpillar (Bug) → Veiled chrysalis (Bug) → Psyche butterfly (Bug/Fairy)

- **Role:** early bug
- **Myth:** The Greek psyche means both "soul" and "butterfly", and the Psychic type takes its name from it. In the tale of Psyche and Eros, a mortal girl survives Aphrodite's trials and is made immortal with butterfly wings.
- **Design:** A caterpillar, then a chrysalis shaped like a veiled bride, then a butterfly whose wings are a soft veil with eye-spots. Fast to evolve, like every early-route bug.
- **Lives in:** Elaia's olive groves and Route 1, mornings.

#### #018–020 Olive cutting (Grass) → Olive sapling (Grass) → Sacred olive (Grass/Fighting)

- **Role:** early grass, intro Pokémon
- **Myth:** Athena won Athens with the gift of the olive. When the Persians burned her sacred olive on the Akropolis, it put out a new shoot a cubit long the next day (Herodotus 8.55). Herakles cut his club from wild olive.
- **Design:** A little olive cutting with two leaves (olives grow from cuttings), then a sapling, then a gnarled ancient trunk whose arm-branches are heavy as Herakles's club. It regrows what it loses.
- **Lives in:** Elaia's olive groves and the Akademia grove; Astyra's Akropolis (rare).
- **Notes:** Could replace Smoliv as Daphne's intro Pokémon.

#### #021–022 Worker ant (Bug) → Myrmidon (Bug/Fighting)

- **Role:** early bug
- **Myth:** When plague emptied Aegina, Zeus turned its ants into men for King Aeacus. They were the Myrmidons, from myrmex, "ant", and they followed Achilles to Troy (Ovid, Metamorphoses 7).
