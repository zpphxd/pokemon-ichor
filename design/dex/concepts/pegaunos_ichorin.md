# Pegaunos and Ichorin: concepts

Three concepts per species for the legendary tier (see "The legendary tier" in `design/dex/SPRITES.md`). Each is a 64×64 battle front, facing left, at about 70% finish. Pick one per species, and that concept gets its own full render pass.

## Pegaunos #148 (Electric/Flying, Storm Steed)

**A: Thunderhead Rampant** (`pegaunos_a.png`)
The brief's stallion, scaled up to box art: a marble-white horse rearing on bronze hooves that throw sparks. Its mane and tail are forks of gold lightning, and Athena's golden bridle crosses its face. The wings are thunderheads, white cumulus along the arm that darkens to storm at the primaries, with lightning crackling between the feathers. This is the classic reading, and its rearing V of wings says Pegasus at any size.

**B: Keraunophoros, the Bolt-Bearer** (`pegaunos_b.png`)
Pegasus as Corinthian coins and black-figure vases show him: a war-steed in a flying gallop, archaic sickle wings curling forward over his back. His coat is black vase glaze whose incised lines glow like heated clay, and he wears a bronze chamfron and peytral under a red hoplite crest. His wings are Zeus's thunderbolts themselves: golden blades with white-hot zigzag cores. He reads as a living vase painting carrying the sky king's lightning.

**C: Gorgonborn** (`pegaunos_c.png`)
The darker myth: the son of Poseidon and Medusa, sprung from her severed neck. A pearl-and-lilac horse with a mane and tail of shadow-cobras, whose gorgon-yellow eyes paralyse, drives a hoof into rock. The spring of Hippocrene bursts up, charged with lightning, while its wings are mantled like a cloak. It's the eeriest of the three and the only one that tells the spring story, but its silhouette is busier.

## Ichorin #152 (Psychic, First Myth)

**A: The First Drop** (`ichorin_a.png`)
The brief made iconic: one drop of ichor, round below and rising to a point that curls into an Ionic volute. It has big calm eyes, two tiny arms, and a trickle of a tail sealed by Talos's bronze nail. It glows from within like liquid gold, motes orbit it, and it hovers over ripples on the Pool of Mnemosyne, where it surfaces in the game. The silhouette is as simple as Mew's or Jirachi's, so it can carry a logo, a title screen or an icon.

**B: The Marble Child** (`ichorin_b.png`)
A tiny divine child of Parian marble come alive. It has the archaic smile, a beaded gold fillet on its brow, little Hypnos wings at its temples and a sun-disc halo. One vein of glowing ichor runs from its heart down its long tail to a sealing bronze nail, as in the Talos myth: the gods are statues with gold for blood. Its Mew body plan reads as a mythical, but it is more sculpture than creature.

**C: Protogonos, the First-Born** (`ichorin_c.png`)
The first myth taken literally. In the Orphic creation, Night laid an egg, and golden-winged Phanes hatched from it as the first light while the shell's halves became sky and earth. Ichorin floats between the two drifting halves of a star-filled shell: a gold being with four little wings and a spark on its brow, with a starlight serpent coiled around the egg. It is the most cosmic and mysterious of the three, but it is busier at 1x, and the shell halves would have to become part of the creature in battle.

## Artist's pick

- **Pegaunos: B.** It has the most distinctive silhouette, the most Greek iconography and the clearest thunderbolt idea. A is the safe crowd-pleaser.
- **Ichorin: A.** It is lovable, iconic at 1x and true to the brief, which suits the title Pokémon. C is the most mysterious alternative.
