# Moirai concepts: Nemira, Lotira, Thanira

Three directions for the whole trio. In each one the three Fates share one body plan and one design language, and the spindle, rod and shears are part of the anatomy, not props they carry. The golden thread is the trio's link in every direction. It begins at Nemira, runs through Lotira from edge to edge, and ends cut at Thanira. Every Fate also carries the trio's pink brow-gem.

## A. Daughters of Night

In Aristophanes' *Birds*, black-winged Night lays the first egg of the world, and Hesiod names the Moirai as Night's daughters. Here they are her three great birds. All three share a swan's neck, a crested head, two plated wings with black-figure flight feathers, and a bronze whorl set in the breast. From that whorl the golden thread runs as a glowing seam down the neck and spine. The tail is the instrument.

- **Nemira:** a soft, rose-white young bird with fleece curls for a crest and night still lingering on her wingtips. Her tail feathers twist into a spindle, with a bronze whorl ring and a fine point, and the thread spins off its tip.
- **Lotira:** a stately pale crane with a straight quill crest and evenly ruled olive flight feathers. Her tail is one ruler-straight plume, banded in bronze at every cubit, and the thread runs taut along it.
- **Thanira:** a gaunt night-violet bird with a feathered hood drawn over a heavy-lidded eye, ragged knife-edged wings and a hooked beak. Her tail splits into two steel blade feathers that cross like shears, and the thread is cut where they meet.

## B. Watchers in the Dark

Fate as something that watches unseen. Science already named these moths for the Fates: the death's-head hawkmoth is *Acherontia atropos*, the geometers (Geometridae) are the "earth-measurers", and the silk moth spins thread. All three share a furred head with large dark eyes, a fleece ruff, a watching eye-spot on each wing, and an abdomen that spins the golden thread.

- **Nemira:** the silk moth: fleece-white, round and soft, with feathery comb antennae and cupped wings edged in pink. Her plump abdomen tapers into a spindle, whorl included, and spins gold silk.
- **Lotira:** the geometer, with broad flat wings ruled along their edges like a measure and crossed by even olive bands. Her straight comb antennae stand like the legs of a pair of dividers, and her abdomen is a straight notched rod with the thread along it.
- **Thanira:** the death's-head hawkmoth: dark fur, a skull on the back drawn like a black-figure mask, and a gold-banded abdomen. Her two long forewings open like shear blades over a tattered violet veil, and the thread is cut between them.

## C. Daughters of Necessity

In Plato the Fates are daughters of Ananke, Necessity, and in the Orphic tales Ananke is a serpent coiled around the world. Here the sisters are celestial wyrms whose bodies are the thread itself. All three share one head with swept veil-fins and one golden seam running down every body. How the body lies is the instrument.

- **Nemira:** pink, with a fleece mane. She is coiled tight around her own tail like thread wound on a spindle, and the tail runs out below as the shaft, with a whorl-fin and a point.
- **Lotira:** limestone. She lies folded in three ruler-straight, equal lengths, an equal share each, banded every cubit, with evenly ruled sail-fins along her back.
- **Thanira:** night-violet. She rears with a starred hood whose two halves run down into blades that cross beneath her chin like shears, and her tail ends in a clean cut of violet fire.

## Notes

- **Retired drafts.** Two earlier drafts were dropped because they read as props bolted onto figures. One was vase-painted maidens holding their tools; the other was faces set into giant instruments.
- **Overlaps to watch.**
  - B shares "moth" with Ikaroth (#100). Keep B furred and veiled, with no feathers or wax.
  - C shares "serpent" with Melodrakon, Pythoros and Lernathan. C-Nemira coiled on her spindle-tail is closest to Melodrakon coiled on its tree.
  - A avoids Kairazor's balance scales, and none of the three directions is a quadruped trio, which the Lazarus avoid-list rules out.
- **Strongest: A.** It has the most legendary-scale silhouette: raised V-wings fill the frame the way Lugia and Ho-Oh do. It reads as one trio at a glance, and the instrument sits in the tail, where it changes the whole outline. B has the best myth hook (*Acherontia atropos*). C is the weakest at 1x: Lotira's folded body reads as a pattern before it reads as a creature.
