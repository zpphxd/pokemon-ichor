# Pokémon Lazarus: what to avoid

Pokémon Lazarus is by Nemo622, who also made Emerald Seaglass. Version 1.0 came out around November 2025 and v2.0 on 2026-02-12. It is the closest existing Greek-themed Emerald hack.

Most of what follows comes from fan walkthroughs ([MrWalkthroughs](https://mrwalkthroughs.com/pokemon-lazarus/walkthrough/)), [pokemonlazarus.net](https://pokemonlazarus.net/) and a docs mirror. The official Ko-fi, ModDB and Fandom pages were blocked.

## What it is

**Setting**
- Modern region with Greek flavor, called Ilios.
- Has Pokémon Centers, shops, a Trainer School, Contests, factories and Acrisia University.
- Theme: Lazarus taxa and fossils.

**Places**
- Acrisia, Jusmail, Kalami, Pythios Town, Sofos, Myrrini, Marmaro, Fresco Isles, Palati.
- Satyr's Lair, Kaptara, the Tower of Dioxippus, Port Pello, Areios, Pentepetal.
- Ruins of Ahiyawa.
- Post-game area: Wakewater Isle.

**Story**
- Team Chimera and a "Mega Plan" that turns Mega Evolution into a weapon.
- Professor Elia, plus Rania and Niko.
- Rayquaza is central, and a Legendary Beast trio appears.
- Kalliope, the Muse of Beauty and Gym 8 leader, turns out to lead Chimera.

**Gyms and League**
- The gyms are run by the Muses.

  | Gym | Leader | Type |
  |---|---|---|
  | 1 | Polymnia | Grass/Bug |
  | 2 | Ourani | Rock/Steel |
  | 3 | Kleio | Ghost/Psychic |
  | 4 | Terpsikore | Fighting/Psychic |
  | 5 | Euterpe | Poison/Water |
  | 6 | Thalia & Melpomene | Electric/Ground |
  | 7 | Rhadine | Fire/Flying |
  | 8 | Kalliope | Dragon/Fairy |

- The gym leaders become the Elite Four. The Champion is Anyalios.

**Starters**
- Nine, unlocked in tiers:
  - Rowlet, Litten, Popplio
  - Chespin, Fennekin, Froakie
  - Sprigatito, Fuecoco, Quaxly

**Systems**
- About 400 Pokémon from Gen 1–9.
- DexNav, day and night encounter tables, followers, level caps.
- Megas, including new ones.
- Berry mutation, fishing and mining mini-games, 60+ side quests, outfits.

**Art**
- GBC (Gold/Silver) style.

**Reception**
- Praised for its story and original region.
- Criticized as "rough around the edges" and easy even on Hard.

**Unverified claim**
- ModDB promo text mentions "Mythic" forms such as Gryphon Arcanine, Medusa Milotic, Ares Scizor, Titan Zapdos and Aegis Lugia. The walkthroughs never mention them.

## Rules for Ichor

Avoid:
- Muse gym leaders.
- Chimera as a villain team.
- Plots that turn Mega Evolution into a weapon.
- A Greek-named legendary beast trio.
- Rayquaza as the central legendary.
- Lazarus's nine starters.
- "Aegis/Gryphon/Medusa/Titan"-prefixed custom forms.
- Town names close to theirs. This is why our Delphi city is **Delphoi** and not Pytho.

Open space:
- An ancient-era setting.
- The Twelve Olympians.
- A Titan villain team.
- The Fates.
- The Underworld.
- The Labors of Heracles.
- The Odyssey.
- The Olympics as a Battle Frontier.
- Laurel badges.
