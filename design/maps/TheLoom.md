# TheLoom

`MAP_THE_LOOM`, 34×14, `MAPSEC_DELPHOI`, under the Omphalos. Beat III-9 (`design/phase4/SCRIPT.md`), and III-10A after the cut. Tileset `loom` (`design/phase4/TILES_loom.md`).

- **Sentence:** a cavern under the navel of the world where one warp-weighted loom, wall to wall, hangs every life in Aethos as a white thread over a black chasm, and every thread is knotted off in red on the same last row.
- **Land:** a pocket of raw Parnassos limestone, the roof low and dark; the floor is a shelf along the south wall that drops sheer into the chasm. Three tongues of rock reach out over the drop to the warp, one under each Fate, and two rock pillars rise out of the dark to carry the loom's posts.
- **Settlement logic:** nobody lives here. The first Archon set the loom over the chasm so the cloth could fall forever; the Fates stand on the three spurs because their chains reach no further: the Spinner's to the west post, the Measurer's up to the beam, the Cutter's to the east post. The stair from the Omphalos comes in at the east end, high on the shelf; Phaedon has set his case down where it arrives.
- **Reveal:** the stair opens on the east end with only the post and the chains in view; the camera pans west across the whole width, so the warp unrolls screen by screen before you get control: thread after thread, all ending on the same red line.
- **Signature:** the woven band at the top of the warp is the story so far, in black-figure on the old red-brown wool (ships, the olive, the bull, the sickle), and below it the bare warp ends in a row of red knots and clay loom-weights over nothing. No other map has cloth.

After the cut (`FLAG_ICHOR_CUT_THE_CHAINS`): the chains hang broken from the posts and the beam, the threads are gray and still, the roof at the west end is torn open on gray sky, and the foot of the Stair of Hours stands at the west end on the shelf, its first step out of reach until a Fate is roused.
