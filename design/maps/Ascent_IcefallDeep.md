# Ascent_IcefallDeep, the coldest dark

Builder: `tools/ichor/maps/ascent_glacier.py` (`build_deep`, gTileset_Chion, 34×28, dark: Hecate's Torch).

- **Sentence:** under the Icefall, a black tarn of Styx water lies in the dark, and one fall the cold never stopped pours into it.
- **Land:** a round chamber of ice round the tarn, an island in its middle, a drowned shrine's niche, a channel north to the meltwater fall and a high pool above it.
- **Settlement logic:** nobody lives here; one acolyte (Hesychios) came down for the quiet. The shrine is Styx's, because the gods swore by this water.
- **Reveal:** the Torch's circle finds the water's edge before it finds anything else; then the sound of the fall.
- **Signature:** the living meltwater fall in a frozen mountain.
