# ThyrsaCity_Agora

- **Sentence:** a wine-and-mask market hall behind Thyrsa's stoa: you buy under a striped awning with a krater of the god's wine at your elbow.
- **Land:** one 12x10 hall, sandstone flags, a meander-frieze back wall; two columns carry the stoa's inner colonnade down the room.
- **Settlement logic:** the merchant's stall stands against the back wall facing the door; wine jars and the krater cluster by the stall, grain baskets on its other side; a tasting table with stools and a goods table sit between the columns; mask busts hang on the east wall.
- **Reveal:** from the door, the awning stall dead ahead between the two columns.
- **Signature:** the Dionysian krater and the wall of masks, Thyrsa's trade.

Built 2026-11-16 (`tools/ichor/maps/polish/thyrsa_agora.py`) from Civic pieces whose backgrounds are sandstone, so it differs from Akti's terracotta agora. The merchant (5, 3) still serves over the counter at row 4; the exit mat stays at (6, 9).
