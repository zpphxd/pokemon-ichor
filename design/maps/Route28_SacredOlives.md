# Route28_SacredOlives

Real-world model: the sacred plain of Krisa and Kirrha below Delphi, the
Pleistos valley's "sea of olives", which it was sacrilege to farm (the First
Sacred War was fought over it). Parnassos rises to the north; the Phaedriades
shine over the last screen.

- **Sentence:** an old road through the god's untouched olives, climbing in low terraces from Kirrha's harbor to the mouth of the Kastalian gorge, with Titans marching through in step.
- **Land:** a plain falling gently south toward the gulf, stepped by dry-stone terrace walls (pezoules) into long strips of olives; to the north the first limestone slopes of Parnassos; in the east the plain pinches between two spurs where the Pleistos's side-stream comes out of the gorge.
- **Settlement logic:** nothing is built on the god's land except what serves it: a roadside altar of Apollo where two ancient trunks squeeze the road, the warden's hut against the oldest olive (where a spring was led down from the Kastalian), herms and stelai at the gates. The road keeps to the terrace tops; grass grows unscythed between the rows because nobody may cut it.
- **Reveal:** after the hollow before the east gate, the road turns north and the whole of the Phaedriades stands over the gorge's mouth in one screen.
- **Signature:** the oldest olive, a vast hollow trunk with Hieron's hut under its crown, and rows of olives that never line up because nobody has ever replanted them.
