# StairOfHours

`MAP_STAIR_OF_HOURS`, 24×110, `MAPSEC_DELPHOI`. Beat III-11 (`design/phase4/SCRIPT.md`, Part 5 "The Stair of Hours"). Tileset `loom` (`design/phase4/TILES_loom.md`).

- **Sentence:** twelve hour-stones hanging in a stopped sky, joined by steep flights of pale steps that zigzag up out of the split Omphalos toward the Lord of Time.
- **Land:** there is no land: a sea of cloud far below, Delphoi's dawn caught in it, and the gray sky all round. The foot is the top of the cracked Omphalos; every landing is a slab of stone floating free, its broken underside showing, its sundial's shadow stopped on its own hour.
- **Settlement logic:** the stair is time itself climbing: the lowest hours are the oldest stone (cyclopean blocks, dark and rough), the middle hours are cut limestone, the high hours white marble, and the twelfth is marble veined with gold. Each landing's riser carries its hour in Greek numerals (Α to ΙΒ), so you always know how far you've come. Flights alternate east and west, so the climb swings back and forth across the sky.
- **Reveal:** from the twelfth flight the landing opens wide, and above it the Lord of Time fills the sky with the Harpe in its grip; later, from the top, the camera looks down the whole height of the fallen stair to the hour-stones hanging one under another.
- **Signature:** the sundials. Twelve dials, twelve stopped shadows, the only clocks in Aethos; on the twelfth chime the last shadow moves.

Gates (P5): each roused Fate opens four landings in the order roused; a closed flight is blocked by its own "held breath" step. Heal sparks at landings 4, 8 and 11, and at 12 after the crumble. Frozen Titanborn on landings 1, 2, 3 and 5 (Petros and Amathos). Items on landings 3, 7 and 10.
