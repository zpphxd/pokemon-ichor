# Ascent_CrystalVault, the hall of frozen ichor

Builder: `tools/ichor/maps/ascent_glacier.py` (`build_vault`, gTileset_Chion, 24×20).

- **Sentence:** behind the Icefall's chained door, the ichor the Giants spilled from Zeus's shield stood up as crystal, and one of it is alive.
- **Land:** a single domed hall of crystal geodes, a crack in the roof running all the way up to the Glacier, a shelf high in the north-west wall.
- **Settlement logic:** a treasure-hunter (Argyros) came down the crack and can't get back up; the careful inscription is a pilgrim's.
- **Reveal:** the Labrys cuts the chain and the whole hall is gold light.
- **Signature:** the crystal giant, Krystolos, asleep in its alcove (a one-time Lv 64 encounter).

## What's here

- **Relics:** the Labrys (the door, on the Icefall side), Alethea's Trowel (a fixed dig: an ostrakon, fixed index 0x38), and, new on 2026-11-18, **Ariadne's Thread**: the crack's ledge can only be reached down the Thread from the Glacier's crevasse; it holds **Choice Specs** and a hidden **Pythian Laurel** (Bottle Cap), and the Thread climbs back up. The builder asserts the ledge is unreachable from the floor.
- Finds: Life Orb, Icy Rock, Ice Stone, hidden Never-Melt Ice and Star Piece. Argyros (Pagoshard, Krystolos). Wild 60–63: Krystolos and Pagoshard.

## Hooks for later

- **The crack in the roof** ("no one is climbing up that without a crook"): a Pan's Crook climb back to the Glacier, for the post-game.
