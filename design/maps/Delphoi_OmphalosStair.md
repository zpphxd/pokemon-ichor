# Delphoi_OmphalosStair

Real-world model: none survives: the chasm under the adyton that ancient
writers imagined, drawn as rock older than the temple cut into one long
switchback stair.

- **Sentence:** a stair of pale stone going down and down in the dark toward a ticking.
- **Land:** a narrow shaft in the rock; the stair runs down one side, turns at a landing, and runs down the other.
- **Settlement logic:** cut by no one anybody remembers; the only things on it are what the night left: Hyperion's dropped torch, a charred knot of the Omphalos's net, and at the bottom the carving of three women and a loom.
- **Reveal:** the carving near the bottom, read without letters.
- **Signature:** the silence broken only by the music box's tick, faster as you go down.
