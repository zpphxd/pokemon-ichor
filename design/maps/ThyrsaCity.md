# ThyrsaCity

- **Sentence:** a walled city of Dionysus on its plain, the Kadmeia raised behind towered walls, the dead lining the road out of the south gate.
- **Land:** a flat Boeotian plain; the upper city (Kadmeia, temple) sits a terrace above the lower town, held by the cross wall and its central stair. Olive groves and scrub close in on the west (Kopais side) and east; the plain opens south.
- **Settlement logic:** houses, agora and Asclepeion inside the walls; outside them only what belongs outside: the necropolis on the south road, groves with a cleared strip along the wall, and the vineyard beyond the Proitid gate where the east road ends.
- **Reveal:** through the south gate the agora and fountain, then the stair up to the temple framed by the north wall's towers.
- **Signature:** the vineyard on the plain past the Proitid gate, and the street of tombs: stelai, naiskos tombs and cypress either side of the south road.

Polish (2026-11-16, `tools/ichor/maps/polish/thyrsa.py`): grid-planted trees outside the walls became staggered olive and pine masses with cypress pairs and lone trees; flowers moved from confetti into beds at doors, the Semele ruin and the Dirke spring; three towers break the north wall's inner face (26-cell edge, now 16 at most).
