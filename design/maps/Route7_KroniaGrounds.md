# Route 7, the Kronia grounds

Built by `tools/ichor/maps/kopais.py` on the Skiron tileset, like Mt. Kithairon below it; `thyrsa.py` still writes its six trainers' parties. Renders: `img/route7_before.png`, `_pass1`, `_pass2`, `_after` (with `_after_views`, `_after_walk`).

## Brief

- **Sentence:** off Kithairon's col, through pine and scree, into the festival field outside Thyrsa's south gate, where the Titanborn masters serve bread at the Kronia.
- **Land:** the col's pines; a scree band with a cut stair; a scree meadow; the walled festival field; olives up to the gate.
- **Settlement logic:** the camp sits on the last flat ground before the city, behind a wall with one gate, the road through its middle.
- **Reveal:** up the stair, the camp's tents and the bread carts across the field.
- **Signature:** the Kronia camp: tents, carts, cookfires, the baker's free bread.

## The walk

Euxenos at the stair's head, Doris on the scree road, Hages in the camp gate (all three can't be walked round). Manto, Myrton and Philiskos are optional. Forced grass: 2 + 3 + 3. The baker heals and gives two Barley Cakes once (unchanged).
