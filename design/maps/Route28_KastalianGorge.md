# Route28_KastalianGorge

Real-world model: the ravine of the Kastalian stream between the two
Phaedriades (Rodini and Phlemboukos) above the Pleistos valley, where the
spring rises and the water falls in steps toward the plain; here filled with the
Titans' last muster.

- **Sentence:** a holy ravine turned into an army camp, climbing past a guarded cutting to two waterfalls that only the Horn of Achelous can climb.
- **Land:** a north-south cleft in pale limestone. The stream comes down the middle, white and loud; on the east bank terraces step up beside it; the west bank is a narrow shelf of laurel and pine reached by a plank bridge. The gorge pinches to two tiles at the cutting, opens to a pool between the lower and upper falls, and ends at the upper fall under the cliffs.
- **Settlement logic:** the camp sits where there is flat ground and water: tents on the lower terraces of both banks, cookfires and a bread oven by the lane, a rope corral on the west bank, the quartermaster's tent apart on a side terrace. Above the camp only the soldiers on watch: a Gigas in the cutting and a sentry on the ledge. The great herm stands at the foot of the falls, where the road ends and the water begins.
- **Reveal:** past the cutting, the lower fall comes into view with the herm in front of it, the first time the climb's goal is seen whole.
- **Signature:** the muster: rows of brown hide tents under sickle banners in a holy gorge, with racks of shovels nobody uses.
