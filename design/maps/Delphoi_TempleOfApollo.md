# Delphoi_TempleOfApollo

Real-world model: the fourth-century Doric Temple of Apollo at Delphi: the
pronaos with its inscriptions (KNOW THYSELF, NOTHING TOO MUCH, the E), a long
cella, and the sunken adyton over the chasm where the Pythia sat on the tripod.

- **Sentence:** a dark temple in which you carry the god's light: one beam from the roof passed by bronze mirrors through four chambers and down into the adyton, where it becomes fire.
- **Land:** one long axis, south to north, rising chamber by chamber; the adyton is sunk below the last chamber, its floor bare rock with vents breathing up from the chasm.
- **Settlement logic:** the pronaos is a porch with a high window; the cella is divided by bronze-grilled screens, each chamber holding a kind of dedication (tripods, lyres, the bronze serpent, laurels); the adyton is the oldest room, rock and fire.
- **Reveal:** when the tripod catches, the Pythia is there on it, where she has sat in the dark all along.
- **Signature:** the beam drawn on the floor as it is passed mirror to mirror, gold by day and silver by night.
