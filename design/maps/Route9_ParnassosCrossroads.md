# Route 9, the Schiste (`Route9_ParnassosCrossroads`)

Built by `tools/ichor/maps/gulf.py` (plan in `gulf_plans.py`). 32x58, aethos + skiron. North: Lebadeia (x 10-13, offset 0; the top six rows are aethos only). South: Kirrha (x 14-17, offset 0). The Korykian Cave (`Route9_KorykianCave`) opens off it.

## Brief

- **Sentence:** from Lebadeia the road comes over Parnassos's shoulder at the three-ways where Oedipus met Laius, then steps down three cliff tiers and through the olive sea to Kirrha.
- **Land:** a narrow valley on the shoulder, a goat path up to a limestone crag, three terrace cliffs stepping south, olive groves on the plain at the bottom.
- **Settlement logic:** the crossroads tomb where the roads meet; a shepherds' camp at the only spring on the slope; pilgrims on the road down to Delphoi's port.
- **Reveal:** the lookout on tier 3, where the sign and Theano point up the mountain at Delphoi.
- **Signature:** Laius's tomb at the three-ways, with the chariot linchpin.

## Walk-through

1. **The three-ways** (rows 6-14). The Daulis road west is lost in thorns (Sickle) past a milestone: PP Up and a hidden Revive. The goat path east climbs to the Korykian crag and the cave mouth (29, 8); Deinon (optional) watches it. Laius's tomb (mound and stele) by the road, a hidden Star Piece in the mound.
2. **The narrows.** Polyon in a niche (14, 16) cuts the road; band 1 (3 deep) right after him.
3. **Tier 1's cliff** steps down east of the cut stair (12-13).
4. **Tier 2: the shepherds' camp.** Tents, a fire, the spring (the midpoint rest: a heal and two Fresh Waters). Philta (optional). The scree pocket behind rubble (24, 29; the Hammer) holds an Ultra Ball. Mnason (5, 27) cuts the bend; band 2 (3 deep) after him.
5. **Tier 3: the long traverse.** Akeson (15, 37) cuts it; band 3 (4 wide) after him. The lookout east: the Delphoi sign, Theano (optional), a goat track that drops by a ledge (30, 42) into the olive sea (one way) with an Ether under it. The ravine west is shut by a great boulder.
6. **The olive sea.** Eryx (16, 51) in a gap in the trees; band 4; the south sign.

Forced grass 13 (bands 1-4). Trainers 7 (4 on the only road: Polyon, Mnason, Akeson, Eryx). Wild 27-31, four time-of-day tables, a spring fishing/surf table and a rock-smash table.
