# Ascent_1F, the Pierian slopes

Builder: `tools/ichor/maps/ascent.py` (aethos + Skiron, 48×40). The brief and the render passes 1–5 are in `design/LEAGUE.md`.

- **Sentence:** a mule road switchbacks up Olympus's pine-clad foot, three terraces above the sea, to where the first cloud sits on the mountain.
- **Land:** the mountain rises north-west; a limestone sea cliff over the Thermaic gulf; two cut banks step the slope into three terraces; a dry torrent bed of white stones.
- **Settlement logic:** the Archon's processional road, paved at the bends; the guard's beacon tower on the one knoll that sees the gulf; stelai of fallen climbers at the bends.
- **Reveal:** the first leg runs the cliff with the open sea below; at the top of the third terrace the road walks into a wall of cloud.
- **Signature:** the challengers' beacon tower, and the stelai of the fallen.

## What's here

- Five trainers (Philon and Klytos hold the coast road as a pair), forced grass bands on every terrace, wild 54–58.
- Finds: Full Restore, Ultra Ball, Max Repel, a Shiny Stone behind a thorn thicket (Demeter's Sickle), hidden Stardust.
- Relics: the Sickle (the thicket), the Girdle (a drum on the bend).
- Shrine: fragment I of the Olympian hymn, at the Muses' spring.

## Hooks for later
