# Arkadia

- **Sentence:** a shepherds' upland town in a cypress wood, its Temple of Artemis raised in a walled precinct over a terraced square.
- **Land:** a basin in the hills: woods all round, a waterfall from the eastern rock into the pool, the upper street a terrace above the square; the roads west and south end against limestone.
- **Settlement logic:** houses and the agora along the upper street; the temple on its podium in its temenos (wall behind, sacred grove beside, columns at the stair head), its altar outside across the road on the axis; the pasture fold by the goatherd's house.
- **Reveal:** down the north road the square opens and the temple's pediment rises over the terrace wall.
- **Signature:** the temple podium with its propylon columns and the altar facing its stair.
