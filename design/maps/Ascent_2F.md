# Ascent_2F, the cloud ledges

Builder: `tools/ichor/maps/ascent.py` (aethos + Skiron, 40×44, `WEATHER_FOG_HORIZONTAL`). Brief and passes 1–3 in `design/LEAGUE.md`.

- **Sentence:** inside the cloud, a narrow road clings to bare cliff faces, ledge by ledge, past the camp of the Titans' lost followers.
- **Land:** the timberline: grey limestone bands, scree fans, a few wind-bent pines; the sea far below through the fog.
- **Settlement logic:** the only flat ground is an overhang with a spring, so the ex-Titanborn camp is there.
- **Reveal:** the firelit camp halfway up; above it the cliffs end at the Icefall's mouth.
- **Signature:** the camp under the overhang: the climb's one midway rest.

## What's here

- The ledge pair (Agathe and Lysandra on the one-wide ledge) and three more trainers; wild 55–59, where the first Ice Pokémon appear (Borapteryx, Chionyad; Niflake and Kryopuff rare).
- **The midpoint shrine:** the camp's cook heals the party and gives Leftovers; resting registers the Ascent's travel herm (the herm rule: `FLAG_ICHOR_TOWN_HERM_ASCENT`, landing (4,41)).
- Finds: Max Revive, PP Max, hidden Big Nugget and Elixir; the Bronze Hammer's rubble. Shrine: fragment II of the hymn.

## Hooks for later

- None on this floor beyond the climb itself: its secrets are the Icefall's.
