# Route 25, up the Eurotas

- **Sentence:** the valley road from the harbor to the town without walls, between the reedy Eurotas and the dark foot of Taygetos, past Apollo's hill at Amyklai.
- **Land:** the river winds up the east side and spreads into reed beds; the west side is barley and olive terraces climbing to Taygetos's foot; a side stream from the mountain crosses at a ford; Amyklai is a low hill with a rock face; plane trees close the top before the road turns west.
- **Settlement logic:** cadets cut reeds where the river is shallow, drill at the ford, run the road; the farm sits in the west fields under the mountain; Amyklai's sanctuary on its hill with the spring beside the throne and the altar in front of it; the Vapheio tomb cut into the hill's flank, older than any of it.
- **Reveal:** at the top the plane trees open and Lakonia lies on the valley floor, no wall anywhere (III-5C).
- **Signature:** the reed beds in the river, with red-cloaked boys knee-deep in them.
