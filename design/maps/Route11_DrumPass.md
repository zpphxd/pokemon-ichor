# Route 11, the drum pass (`Route11_DrumPass`)

Built by `tools/ichor/maps/gulf.py` (plan in `gulf_plans.py`). 30x52, aethos + skiron. North: Aigion (x 13-16, offset 0). South: Styx Falls (x 11-12, offset -1; the bottom six rows are aethos only).

## Brief

- **Sentence:** up the Selinous gorge beside its river, past the nymphs' spring and the waterfall, to the pass where a fallen temple's column drums block the road; then down into the bowl of Styx Falls.
- **Land:** Aigion's olive terraces, a cliff lip with a waterfall, the gorge with its river and gravel banks between limestone crags, a second cliff, a cutting through the pass, the fallen temple's floor, cypress down to Styx.
- **Reveal:** the column drum at the cutting's mouth.
- **Signature:** the river gorge with its ford, its waterfall and the nymphs' niches.

## Walk-through

1. **The terraces.** Dorys, Aigion's watch (16, 6), cuts the road; band 1.
2. **The gorge's lip:** the stair down (14-15); the waterfall (4-5) beside it.
3. **The gorge (required).** The east bank road between the river and the crags. Thraseas (11, 16) cuts the bank; the ford west (17) to the west bank (Xanthe, optional; a Hyper Potion, a hidden Revive). The nymphs' niches and the spring keeper (14, 19): a heal and a Mystic Water. Lachon (11, 21) cuts the bank; band 2 (3 deep) after him. The rubble pocket (15, 26; the Hammer): an Ultra Ball. An Achaean watchtower on the rim.
4. **The pass.** The stair (9-10), Myrine (14, 35) at the cutting, the column drum (12, 37): the Girdle pushes it out into the temple's floor. Drums and a fallen block lie about; an Ether, a hidden Super Repel, a hero's mound above.
5. **Down to Styx.** Pheron (14, 45) cuts the road; band 3; the south sign.

Forced grass 8 (counted with the drum moved). Trainers 6 (5 on the only road). Wild 29-33, four time-of-day tables, the river's surf and fishing table, a rock-smash table.
