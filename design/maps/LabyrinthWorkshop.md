# Daedalus's workshop (`MAP_LABYRINTH_WORKSHOP`) and Minos's tomb (`MAP_LABYRINTH_MINOS_TOMB`)

## The workshop

Built by `tools/ichor/maps/labyrinth_workshop.py` from the approved scene-D mock-up (`labyrinth_scene.workshop`); tileset `daidaleion`; 24×18, lit.

- **Sentence:** a long vaulted hall of bronze automata tied to their plinths, the maker's benches and plans, and a grated shaft of grey sky.
- **Settlement logic:** the hub at the middle of the house: the back door for Daedalus's own way, the threshold for the front way, the stair down to the Titans, the lift up to the palace stair.
- **Reveal:** scene D: the collar on the bench, the Master Ball and the Labrys.
- **Signature:** the ticking music box, the prototype of Elpis's.
- Models: Daedalus as the Odyssey's and Pindar's maker of living statues; Hephaestus's golden handmaids; a Minoan bronze-worker's shop.

Holds: the heal bed, the Pithos, Number Four, the plans, the lift (after scene D; the Grand Staircase's lift lands on warp 5), scene D (`VAR_ICHOR_KNOSSA` 6 or 7 → 8). Not yet: Daedalus's own sprite (an elder stands in), fossil revival, the Hall of Failures.

## Minos's tomb

Built by `tools/ichor/maps/labyrinth_tomb.py`; 16×12; behind Ring V's cracked wall %1.

- **Sentence:** a low painted crypt cut half into the rock, red columns and double axes round the judge's bier.
- **Model:** the Temple Tomb south of Knossos (pillared crypt, gypsum, a rock-cut inner chamber).
- Scene F word for word; the Helm; a Gold Stater, a hidden Pearl and Obol. Marked `ichor_kind: interior` (one chamber off Ring V).
