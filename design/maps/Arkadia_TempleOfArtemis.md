# Arkadia_TempleOfArtemis

- **Sentence:** an endless-night grove of glades and hedges where the hunt climbs to Kallisto's rock.
- **Land:** three glades stepped north, walled by hedge and cypress; Kallisto's sanctum on a raised rock at the top, reached by a stair cut into its face.
- **Settlement logic:** the forecourt by the town door holds the kennels, the statue and the altar; the propylon gate leads into the grove; votive stelai mark each glade.
- **Reveal:** the last glade opens under the rock with the stair rising to Kallisto.
- **Signature:** the moonlit clearings where the hounds see farther.
