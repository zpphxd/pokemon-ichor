# Items and doors for the Kalliste slice

What the slice's items look like in the bag, the Agora and on the ground, and how its doors open. Everything is generated by two scripts, so edit the script and rerun it rather than touching the PNGs or palettes.

| Script | Writes |
|---|---|
| `tools/ichor/art/items.py` (with `items_lib.py`) | the item icons and their palettes in `graphics/items/`, and the ground jar `graphics/object_events/pics/misc/ichor_item_jar.png` |
| `tools/ichor/art/doors.py` | the door animations `graphics/door_anims/ichor_*.png` |

```bash
/usr/bin/python3 tools/ichor/art/items.py --write --preview design/phase1/img
/usr/bin/python3 tools/ichor/art/doors.py --write --preview design/phase1/img
make -j$(sysctl -n hw.ncpu) && tools/ichor/smoke_test.sh
tools/ichor/tests/items_doors.sh          # the in-game check below
```

## Item icons

**The look.** Icons are 24×24 Gen 3 icons in the master palette (`PLAN.md`), with an ink outline and light from the top left. Each one stays readable for what it does:
- **HP medicine is a flask.** The ware gives the strength.
- **Status medicine is a herb bundle tied with wool.** The wool's color names the status.
- **PP medicine is a little amphora of nectar.**

They are drawn as shapes first (shaded ellipses, tubes and leaves), then finished by hand with ASCII patches, the same way the sprite scripts work.

**Poké Balls keep their name and look** (director's ruling), so the ball icons are untouched.

**Shared pics and palettes.** Several items share one pic and differ only by palette (Potion, Super Potion and Hyper Potion all use `potion.png`). Two pics can also share one palette: `star.pal` colors both Stardust's heap and the Star Piece. A shared pic is drawn once over a fixed role order, and every palette that colors it is rewritten. Before writing anything, `items.py` reads `src/data/items.h` and checks two things:
- every palette that colors a pic it draws is one it writes;
- every palette it writes colors only pics it draws.

That check is why Star Piece, Comet Shard and Big Pearl were redrawn too. Without them, `star.pal`, `comet_shard.pal` and `pearl.pal` would have scrambled those icons.

**TM and HM colors.** These come from `gTypesInfo[].paletteTMHM`. Each type's seal ramp is built from one base color (`TYPE_COLORS` in `items.py`); the scroll itself stays the same in every palette.

### Flag-only key items: drawn, not yet wired

The slice gives four key things that the engine stores as flags, not as items:
- the Talaria (`FLAG_RECEIVED_RUNNING_SHOES`)
- the Augur's Eye (`FLAG_ICHOR_DEXNAV_GET`)
- the Pokédex (`FLAG_SYS_POKEDEX_GET`)
- the Hearth Laurel (`FLAG_BADGE01_GET`)

So none of them has a bag icon to redraw. Their icons are drawn as ready assets, but nothing references them, so they cost no ROM:

| Asset | Look |
|---|---|
| `icons/ichor_talaria.png` | A laced sandal-boot with a white wing at the ankle and a gold buckle |
| `icons/ichor_augurs_eye.png` | A bronze amulet: wings, an apotropaic eye, and a loop and cord |
| `icons/ichor_codex.png` | The Pokédex as a codex: two wooden tablets with a sketch and notes scratched in the wax, cords and a gold clasp |
| `icons/ichor_hearth_laurel.png` | A laurel sprig with gilded leaf tips, a ribbon, and the hearth's flame at its tip |

Each has a palette of the same name in `icon_palettes/`. Using one needs an item entry in `include/constants/items.h` and `src/data/items.h`, plus its `INCGFX` lines in `src/data/graphics/items.h`.

## The ground item: a sealed clay jar

`OBJ_EVENT_GFX_ITEM_BALL` keeps its name, but it now draws `gObjectEventGraphicsInfo_IchorItemJar`. That is a 16×16 single frame: a small terracotta jar sealed with beeswax, the wax tied with twine, and a black-figure band with linen dots round the shoulder.

- **Palette.** It is drawn in the townsfolk palette (`ichor_folk.pal`, tag `OBJ_EVENT_PAL_TAG_ICHOR_FOLK`), using ink, brown, terra, ochre, gold, the two clay tones and linen.
  - On any map with folk walkers it costs no extra OBJ palette slot.
  - It depends on the folk palette's fixed order (`SPRITES_folk.md`). If `FOLK_COLORS` changes, rerun `items.py --write`.
- **Readability.**
  - It reads as "an item is here" on grass, barley, limestone path, sand, rock floor, Kalliste paving, Akti's quay crates, and the oikos, civic and temple floors (`items_ground_jar.png`).
  - On the terracotta oikos floor it is clay on clay, carried by its outline, band and gold cap.
- **The Poké Ball graphic is untouched.** `gObjectEventGraphicsInfo_PokeBall` still serves `OBJ_EVENT_GFX_POKE_BALL` and the followers' ball. Hoenn maps' item balls become jars too.

**`INVENTORY.md` §1 had the aethos doors half right.** It says `general.png`, `poke_center.png` and `poke_mart.png` play over aethos's `021`, `061` and `041`. In fact `GetDoorGraphics()` matches a door by tileset, and those entries name `gTileset_General`. So on `main`:
- Akti's doors did not animate at all.
- The Hoenn doors that did play were Elaia's, through its scaffold's `gTileset_Petalburg`.

`doors_ingame.png` shows both, before and after.

**Elaia's scaffold** still sits on `gTileset_General` + `gTileset_Petalburg`, so its houses are Hoenn art. Drawing a Greek door over a Hoenn house door would not match its tiles. Its doors now open without an animation instead. When Elaia moves to kalliste, its houses use kalliste's non-animated doors (`211`, `239`, `262`), as Akti's Fisher's house does.

### The aethos doors

aethos keeps General's five animated door ids, so all five are drawn and registered, including the three no slice map uses yet. Each frame is built from the door's own tiles in aethos palette 0:
- the first frame matches the closed door pixel for pixel;
- only the doorway changes.

The open doorway shows a dark room, with daylight on the first floor tiles inside. A turning leaf darkens one shade per frame.

### Engine changes (`src/field_door.c`, all marked `// Ichor:`)

- **`sIchorDoorAnimGraphicsTable`** sits after the vanilla table and holds the five aethos entries. `METATILE_Aethos_Door_*` are local defines, and `gTileset_Aethos` is a local `extern`, so neither `metatile_labels.h` nor `tilesets.h` changes. The palette array has 12 entries because a 1×2 door reads 12.
- **`GetDoorGraphics()`** looks in the Ichor table first. On an Ichor map it looks nowhere else, so no Hoenn door animation can play there; a Hoenn door tile just opens without one, with the plain door sound.
  - `IsIchorDoorMap()` tests `gMapHeader.regionMapSectionId` against `MAPSEC_ELAIA_VILLAGE` … `MAPSEC_NONE`. That range holds as long as new Aethos map sections are appended after Kalliste's.
- **1×1 sheets get 4 blank tiles at the end.** The engine copies 8 tiles a frame whatever the door's size, so this keeps the last frame's copy inside the array.

### For whoever moves Akti to `gTileset_Akti` and Route 1 to `gTileset_Agros`

- **The entries are keyed to `gTileset_Aethos` and its ids.**
  - A layout that keeps aethos as its primary keeps these doors, whatever its secondary.
  - A door redrawn into a new tileset needs its own entry: add a line to `DOORS` in `doors.py` with the metatile ids and the opening box, rerun it, and register the sheet.
- **Leave the secondary's last 8 tile slots (504–511) empty.**
  - A door's frames are copied there while it animates (`DOOR_TILE_START_SIZE1`), so any tile in those slots is overwritten for the moment the door moves.
  - kalliste uses 496 tiles, so it is clear.
- **If a kalliste door should animate,** for example the temple's bronze doors (`2E3`), its behavior must become `MB_ANIMATED_DOOR` in kalliste's attributes. It then needs a sheet and an entry here. The director ruled them non-animated for now.

## In-game check

`tools/ichor/tests/items_doors.sh [rom] [out] [mode]` runs `items_doors.lua` on the headless runner. It patches nothing in the ROM: ids and offsets come from `items_doors_consts.c`, compiled with the game's headers.
1. **Doors.** It walks into the Elaia house and Akademia doors, Akti's Asclepeion and Agora, and out of the Asclepeion and the Agora over their mats, with a screenshot every 3 frames.
   - The three unused aethos doors are written into Akti's live map grid over the Asclepeion's door cell, whose warp they borrow.
   - Opening and closing the bag redraws the field from the grid.
2. **The ground jars**, where the maps place them.
3. **The bag.** Pockets are filled straight into SaveBlock1, with quantities XORed with the encryption key. Then it takes one screenshot per item in every pocket; the Items pocket runs twice, because it holds 30.
4. **The Agora's buy list.**

`mode=doors` runs only part 1, which is how the "before" row of `doors_ingame.png` was taken from `main`'s ROM.

- **Items wild Pokémon may carry in the slice** keep their generic icons: Pretty Feather, Everstone, Light Clay, Metal Coat, Moon Stone, Luminous Moss, Dragon Scale and Poison Barb.
- **The badge art and the trainer card** (`graphics/trainer_card/badges.png`) are not item icons. The Hearth Laurel icon above could seed them.
- **Item names** are unchanged. "Potion" and "Escape Rope" still read fine, and renaming is a text decision.
