# Battle interface

The battle interface is painted on pottery, to match the dialogue box (`graphics/text_window/`, `tools/ichor/art/ui_frames.py`). Every piece is generated by a script in `tools/ichor/art/`, so edit the script and rerun it rather than touching the PNGs.

| Script | Draws |
|---|---|
| `ui_battle.py` | Healthboxes, HP and EXP bars, status badges, party tokens and their ground line, enemy shadows |
| `ui_battle_textbox.py` | The battle text box and its tilemap, the menu and PP text palettes, the level-up plaque, the "press A" arrow |
| `ui_battle_sprites.py` | The ability pop-up, the R (last ball) and L (move info) windows, the Omphalos trigger and mega mark |
| `ui_battle_transitions.py` | The tiles and maps of the two pottery transitions |
| `ui_battle_review.py` | The review sheets below |
| `ui_battle_lib.py` | Shared: the master palette, an indexed canvas, the 3x5 lettering and the meander painters |

## What each element is now

| Element | Look |
|---|---|
| Healthboxes (singles, doubles, both sides, safari) | **Terracotta plaques.** Names and numbers in black-figure ink on a light-clay panel, with a clay rim and a vertical meander strip at each end, like the framed panel of a vase. |
| HP bar | **An ink-drawn tube** with a glaze trough. Laurel green when healthy, sun-gold for caution, fire red for danger. "HP" is lettered in ink beside it. |
| EXP bar | The same tube in Aegean blue, along the bottom rim of the player's box, lettered "EXP". |
| Status badges | Ink-edged pills with parchment letters: PSN nightshade purple, PAR amber, SLP river stone, FRZ and FRB sea ice, BRN fire red. |
| Party balls | **Little kraters** standing on a painted ground line that tapers to dots: clay when healthy, gold when statused, glazed and cracked when fainted, an outline for an empty slot. The "already caught" mark on a wild box is a krater too. |
| Enemy shadows | A glaze core with a dithered ink rim, so the platform shows through. |
| Message box | **The dialogue box itself**: its corner, band, side and field tiles. |
| FIGHT/BAG menu, move list, PP and type | The player's menu frame (frame 1 is the pottery frame) around parchment, with the dialogue box's ink. The cursor is an ink pointer. |
| PP warnings | Darker amber, orange and red, readable on parchment. |
| "Press A" arrow | A plain downward triangle, red with an ink edge, in battle and in the field. The old blunt shield read as a heart at 1x. |
| Ability pop-up | A clay plaque with meander strips: the Pokémon's name in parchment on a black-glaze band, the ability on parchment below. Item pop-ups use it too. |
| R and L windows | Clay tablets with parchment key caps. |
| Mega trigger | **The Omphalos**, Delphi's netted navel stone: dim marble until pressed, then lit with golden ichor. The mega mark beside the level is a small gold omphalos. |
| Level-up plaque | Parchment over light clay, rimmed in clay. |
| Wild transition | **Bands of a turning pot** sweep in from alternate sides: glaze with its meander, then clay with black rays. Then it fades to black. |
| Trainer transition | **A black-figure face-off.** A clay band between meander friezes (rays hang from the glaze shoulder and rise from the foot) sweeps in, the upper half from the left and the lower from the right. The opponent and the player slide onto it as black silhouettes and face each other, as on an Attic amphora. Then it fades to black. |

Left as they are:
- The Z-Move, Dynamax, Tera and Ultra Burst triggers, and the Tera type indicators (`*_indicator.png`). Those gimmicks are off in Ichor.
- The type icons of `B_SHOW_TYPES`, which is set to never show.
- The mugshot, team (Aqua, Magma), legendary and Battle Frontier transitions, which are set pieces.

## How the engine draws it

**Healthbox palette.** One OBJ palette, loaded from `ball_status_bar.png`, colors the boxes, the EXP bar, the badges, the ground line and the enemy shadows. Its slots have fixed jobs:

| Slot | Color | Job |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | ink (32,24,24) | text, meander, outlines |
| 2 | light clay (224,136,88) | lettering ground; the game fills it to erase text |
| 3 | clay (184,88,48) | text shadow, rim |
| 4 | clay dark (120,48,24) | incised lines |
| 5 | parchment (232,216,176) | badge letters |
| 6 | sun-gold (240,192,64) | spare |
| 7 | glaze (64,40,32) | empty EXP trough, shadow core |
| 8, 9 | Aegean light, Aegean | EXP fill |
| 10 | added red (152,32,72) | ♀ (`DYNAMIC_COLOR1`) |
| 11 | Aegean dark (24,56,120) | ♂ (`DYNAMIC_COLOR2`) |
| 12–15 | written at run time | badge color of battlers 0–3, from `sStatusIconColors` in `src/battle_interface.c` |

**HP bar palette.** A second OBJ palette, from `ball_display.png`, colors the HP bar sprite and the party tokens: 1 ink, 2 glaze trough, 3–5 green, 6–8 gold, 9–11 red (light, mid, shade), 12–14 the token clays and 15 parchment.

**Box layout.** The game prints into fixed places, so the art keeps them plain ground:
- the name at (8, 3) on the opponent's box and (16, 3) on the player's;
- the level right-aligned at x 88 or 96;
- the player's HP numbers on row 24, with the badge left of them;
- on the opponent's box and in doubles, the badge sits left of the bar. There, "HP" gives way to `misc_frameend.png`, the bar's left end.

The bar's right end is drawn into the box. In doubles the game swaps it with `*_frameend*.png` when the HP numbers replace the bar. The EXP tiles carry the box's bottom rim, so the player's box ends at y 111, above the text box.

**Text box (BG0).**
- BG palette 0 serves battle messages (ink 1, shadow 6, field 15) and the egg hatch text (ink 5). Battle and evolution messages draw the dark continue arrow (`down_arrow_alt.png`, edge 1, fill 2), so slot 2 is red. The field's arrow is `down_arrow.png` (edge 2, fill 4) in the dialogue box palette.
- Tiles 1–2 are the menu cursor, drawn with palette 0.
- Tiles 0x12–0x1A and 0x22–0x2A are overwritten with the player's menu frame, so the art avoids them.
- BG palette 5 (`text.pal`) is the menus' parchment and ink. Its slots 11–12 are rewritten from `text_pp.pal` by how much PP is left. The summary screen loads `text_pp.pal` too.
- The egg hatch and trade scenes reuse this text box, so they get the pottery box as well.

**Pop-up palette.** `ability_pop_up.pal` also colors the R and L windows:
- 7 is the name color and the parchment band; 9 is the ability color; 1 is the shadow of both.
- The cycle arrows are 8 and 9. To hide them the game copies 13 (the window body) into them; to show them it copies 11 and 10.

**Transitions** (`src/battle_transition.c`, marked "Ichor").
- `BattleTransition_StartOnField` swaps the ordinary transitions (Slice, Poké Balls trail, angled wipes and the rest) for `B_TRANSITION_ICHOR_TRAINER` or `B_TRANSITION_ICHOR_WILD`, by whether it is a trainer battle. `battle_setup.c` is untouched.
- BG0 holds the vase in BG palette 15. Window 0 reveals it one scanline at a time, through HBlank DMA into `WIN0H`, as the mugshot transition does. BG0 drifts left a pixel a frame, like a turning pot.
- The figures are the trainer front pics. Their palettes are repainted at run time: the darkest shades (the outlines) become the band's clay, as if incised through the glaze, and every other color becomes ink. The player is flipped to face the opponent. A second opponent or an in-game partner gets a place on the band too.
- Lengths, from the end of the shared intro blink: wild about 36 frames, as Slice; trainer about 86, a little under the Poké Balls trail.

## Testing

The shots below come from a temporary patch that was never committed. It hooked `ProcessPlayerFieldInput` in `src/field_control_avatar.c`:
- START gave a level-50 party and started a wild battle against an Intimidate Gyarados, for the ability pop-up.
- SELECT started a trainer battle against `TRAINER_ICHOR_KLEON_ELAIA_SNIVY`.
- `gSpecialVar_0x8004` picked the scenario: full HP; caution and danger HP with PSN and BRN; or wild doubles with PAR and SLP. A fourth scenario fought `TRAINER_ICHOR_IONE_1` over the LEADER background.

The lead held Lucarionite, with a Mega Ring in the bag, for the trigger. A Lua script on the headless runner pressed through each battle and took the screenshots.
