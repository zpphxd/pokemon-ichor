# Oikos: the Greek interior primary

`gTileset_Oikos` ("household") replaces vanilla `gTileset_Building` under Ichor's interiors. It is Porytiles-managed, lives in `data/tilesets/primary/oikos/`, and is regenerated by `tools/ichor/art/oikos_paint.py`. No layout uses it yet. The director switches layouts over at merge time.

## The finding that shapes everything

**Building is almost empty.** It holds 8 metatiles: the void, the TV, the PC and the exit mat.
- Every floor, wall, window, stair, bed, chair and table in a Hoenn interior comes from the secondary: BrendansMaysHouse, Lab, PokemonCenter, Shop and GenericBuilding.
- Those secondaries also point at about 20 of Building's tiles by index. These are the caps of their PCs and monitors, a window's glass and the void fill.

**So a repaint of the primary alone barely changes a room.** On the slice's seven layouts (`img/oikos_slice_*.png`):
- the TV becomes Mom's loom
- the Pokémon Center's PC becomes the Pithos
- object caps and one window's glass shift to the master palette.

Everything else is still Hoenn.

**What oikos adds.** To deliver the Greek look the bible asks for (terracotta and marble floors, plastered walls, klinai, amphorae and so on), oikos adds a full interior kit as new primary metatiles. The kit uses tile slots and palettes that no secondary touches. Rooms built from the kit read as Greek straight away (`img/oikos_mock_*.png`, and `img/oikos_ingame_test_2f.png` in-game).

**Notes on the vanilla ids:**
- `METATILE_Building_TV_*` and `METATILE_Building_PC_*` still name these ids, so `tv.c` and `field_specials.c` work unchanged.
- The behaviors are kept. Interacting with the loom runs the TV code, and the Pithos opens storage, which the script wants. Give the loom a bg event, as SCRIPT.md already plans.
- There is no tile animation (callback `NULL`), because Building's TV-on frames would paint Hoenn art over the loom.
- **Conflict.** SCRIPT.md wants the 1F TV to be a loom and the 2F TV to be knucklebones, but both are metatile `0x002`. The primary can only be one of them, so `0x002` is the loom. In the 2F rebuild, use the kit's knucklebones table `0x047`.

## The kit: `0x008`–`0x051`, plus floor variants `0x060`–`0x091`

**Layer types:**
- *normal*: the top layer draws over the player.
- *covered*: both layers draw under the player.

**Wall cells.** Furniture against the back wall sits on the wall: its upper cell on `0x010`, its lower cell on `0x018`.

**Floor variants.** Free-standing furniture sits on terracotta or marble. Each such piece repeats at **id + `0x40`** on the other floor. For example, the klismos `0x036` on terracotta is `0x076` on marble, and the krater `0x044` on marble is `0x084` on terracotta. The variants share the object's tiles, so they cost no tile space.

**Collision is set per map cell in the layout, not here.** Recommended:
- Floors, doorways, stairs, thresholds and mats: passable. Add warp events on doorways and stairs.
- Walls, windows and wall furniture: blocked.
- Furniture cells: blocked, **except** the upper cells marked *normal* below. Those are passable, so the player can walk behind olives, lampstands, columns and statues.

**Palette sources.** All colors are multiples of 8 and come from PLAN.md's families. There are two additions:
- **bronze** (96,64,32) and (168,112,48), alongside sun-gold
- **wood** (72,48,32), (128,88,48) and (176,128,72).

Slots 2–4 were unused magenta in Building. Only Frontier and Facility metatiles referenced them, and those already rendered magenta. **Greek interior secondaries keep slots 6–12 as usual.**

## Budget

- **Tiles.** The kit uses 257 tiles, and 87 free slots remain. A free slot is one that is blank in Building and not referenced by any secondary that pairs with Building.
- **Metatile ids.** `0x092`–`0x1FF` are free.

## Porytiles rules for this tileset

- `porytiles/tilesets/gTileset_Oikos/config.yaml` pins tiles to `patch` and palettes to `locked`, for oikos only.
  - A default `optimize` compile rebuilds tiles.png from oikos's own metatiles. That would drop or move the tiles the Hoenn secondaries use.
  - I tested this: an optimize compile cut tiles.png to 32 tiles. Patch mode never disturbs an existing tile.
- **Edit the art in `oikos_paint.py`, not in `porytiles_src`.**
  - The script writes the Porymap assets in `porytiles_bin` directly, at fixed tile indices.
  - It then runs `decompile-tileset` and a `compile-tileset` that must change nothing.
  - It fails if any secondary-referenced tile moved.
  - A hand edit to `porytiles_src` still compiles safely, in patch mode, into free slots. But the next generator run overwrites it.
- **Import note.** Building's unused palette slots held magenta filler, which Porytiles rejects, so the import copy sets them black.

## Known gaps

- **The statue is small.** It reads as a robed figure with a snake staff at 3×, but it is the weakest piece. SCRIPT.md also makes the Asclepeion's statue an object event, so the tile one is for halls.
- **Some vanilla tiles were not redrawn.** These are only recolored through palettes 0, 1 and 5: the Frontier monitors, the union-room terminals, and the Poké Ball wall panels used by Frontier, Contest and Cable Club secondaries. They are Hoenn-only and on ART_PLAN's cut list. The Cable Club, if kept for trading, needs its own pass.
- **There is no animated hearth or lamp flame.** Animations would need new code in `src/tileset_anims.c`, which is outside this batch.
